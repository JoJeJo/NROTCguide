Where this comes from

Every NROTC unit runs on the Regulations for Officer Development (NSTC M-1533.2F, February 2025). This site pulls the parts midshipmen actually need — program rules, what to know, and what to wear — and cites the specific ROD sections.

When a topic is governed by a separate publication the ROD points to (like uniform regs), that's noted.

Official documents:

  • Regulations for Officer Development (NSTC M-1533.2F, Feb 2025)
  • NROTC Scholarship Application Instructions (May 2026)
  • U.S. Navy Uniform Regulations (NAVPERS 15665J) — via MyNavyHR
  • Marine Corps Uniform Regulations (MCO P1020.34H) — via the Marine Corps Publications Electronic Library
Heads up: Regulations change. Your unit staff always outranks this website. When in doubt, ask your chain of command.
