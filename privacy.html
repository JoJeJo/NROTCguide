Privacy

What this site collects — and what it doesn't.

No data collection

Browsing this site collects nothing: no accounts, no sign-ins, no cookies, no analytics, and no tracking. Nothing you read, click, or search on this site is recorded by us.

Ask a Midshipman

If you submit a question through the Ask form, your question, your email address, and any preferences you select about who should answer (gender, branch, unit, class year) are sent through Formspree, the form service this site uses, which delivers them to our team by email. Your email is used only to send you the answer. It is never published or shared. Formspree handles submissions under its own privacy policy.

Site analytics

This site counts page visits with GoatCounter. It uses no cookies and collects no personal information: only aggregate counts of which pages are visited. No individual visitor is identified. GoatCounter handles this data under its own privacy policy.

External links

This site links to outside pages: official government documents, NROTC unit websites, and Buy Me a Coffee. Those sites operate under their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Donations

Voluntary contributions through Buy Me a Coffee are processed entirely by Buy Me a Coffee under its own terms and privacy policy. We receive no payment details from those transactions.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
