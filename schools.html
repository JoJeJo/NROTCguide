Where you can go

The scholarship has to be used at a school with an NROTC unit. Schools are grouped by unit below. A crosstown affiliate has no unit of its own — its students join the host unit on their card and drill with it. A consortium is not crosstown: it groups two or more host schools whose separate units operate together under one name, like the Chicago Area consortium.
